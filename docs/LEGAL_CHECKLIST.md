# Legal and Policy Checklist

This is a practical project checklist, not legal advice.

## Open-source repository

-   `LICENSE` present.
-   Third-party dependencies have compatible licenses.
-   Required notices/attributions are preserved.
-   Do not copy proprietary code/assets.
-   Confirm rights to contributed images, icons, fonts, and
    documentation.
-   Maintain a code of conduct.
-   Maintain security reporting instructions.
-   Clarify brand/name/logo use separately from source-code licensing.

## Privacy

For self-hosted software, document data flows and operator
responsibilities.

If an official hosted Duesora service launches, obtain
jurisdiction-appropriate legal review for:

-   Privacy Policy;
-   Terms of Service;
-   cookie/analytics disclosures;
-   subprocessors;
-   retention;
-   account deletion;
-   acceptable use;
-   billing/refunds if paid;
-   data processing terms where needed.

## Monitoring

Users should only monitor systems they are authorized to monitor. Avoid
features designed to bypass access controls or probe private networks.

## Branding

Before commercial launch or trademark filing, perform official
trademark/company/domain clearance in target jurisdictions. A repository
name search is not legal clearance.
