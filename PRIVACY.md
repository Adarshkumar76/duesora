# Privacy Principles

This document defines product privacy principles for Duesora. It is not
a substitute for a deployment-specific privacy policy.

## Self-hosted deployments

A self-hoster controls its own deployment and is generally responsible
for its users, infrastructure, retention, backups, and applicable legal
obligations.

## Duesora design principles

-   Collect only data needed for the feature.
-   No telemetry by default.
-   If telemetry is introduced, make it transparent, documented, and
    opt-in.
-   Do not sell user data.
-   Provide export and deletion workflows.
-   Avoid storing third-party passwords.
-   Encrypt sensitive integration credentials.
-   Keep secrets out of logs.
-   Define retention for logs, notifications, audit events, and
    attachments.
-   Make external data transfers visible when integrations are
    configured.

## Hosted service

If an official Duesora-hosted service is launched, publish a separate
legally reviewed privacy policy describing the operator, purposes, legal
bases where applicable, processors, retention, user rights,
international transfers, and contact details.
